# Forges

A **forge** hosts Git remotes and adds issues, merge requests, and CI. GitHub has its own part. This part is the other common forges.

| Chapter | Focus |
|---------|--------|
| **GitLab** | Groups, merge requests, CI YAML, self-host notes |
| **Gitea** | Lightweight self-hosted forge |

Read these after remotes and branching — or jump here if that is what your workplace already runs.
